## Full description (4000 max)
A neon-lit death run, ninety-nine levels deep.

Cracked is a fast room-clearing shooter. Each level is a chain of sealed rooms. The door locks behind you, robots pour through the gates in the far wall, and the exit only opens when the room is clean.

★ 99 LEVELS
Every level has its own colour theme and cover layout. Some add a twist: swarm, crossfire, juggernaut, blackout or overdrive.

★ A BOSS EVERY TENTH LEVEL
Ten boss rooms, with the last one waiting at level 99.

★ THREE GUNS
A rifle, a shotgun and a scoped marksman rifle, with recoil, tracers and hit markers that tell you when you've found a weak point.

★ MOVE FAST
Sprint, slide, jump and dash between cover. Kill quickly to build your combo and your score.

★ BUILT FOR PHONES
Touch controls made for one-thumb movement and aiming, plus full gamepad support.

★ GOOGLE PLAY GAMES
Sign in to keep your progress in the cloud and carry it across phones.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Just you, three guns and ninety-nine rooms of robots.
