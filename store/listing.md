## Full description (4000 max)
A neon-lit death run across a hostile galaxy.

Cracked is a fast room-clearing shooter. Each level is a chain of sealed rooms. The door locks behind you, robots pour through the gates in the far wall, and the exit only opens when the room is clean.

★ A GALAXY TO CONQUER
Every star on the map is a level with its own name, colour theme and cover layout. How many are there? Find out. Some add a twist: swarm, crossfire, juggernaut, blackout or overdrive.

★ BOSSES, THEN MORE BOSSES
Boss stars are scattered across the map. The crown at the galaxy's core is waiting for anyone who makes it that far, and past it lies the abyss: endless waves, almost no healing.

★ AN ARSENAL THAT GROWS
Start with a rifle, a shotgun and a scoped marksman rifle. Conquer stars to unlock SMGs, burst rifles, LMGs, plasma, a railgun and an arc gun, plus frag, EMP and incendiary grenades. Carry three guns at once.

★ BUILD YOUR FIGHTER
Earn coins and XP from every fight and spend them in the Armory: a huge skill tree across three branches (Runner, Gunner, Demolition). Switch your active skills between runs, or try any build in the Practice Range.

★ MOVE FAST
Sprint, slide, jump and dash between cover. Kill quickly to build your combo and your score.

★ BUILT FOR PHONES
Touch controls made for one-thumb movement and aiming, plus full gamepad support.

★ GOOGLE PLAY GAMES
Sign in to keep your progress in the cloud and carry it across phones.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Just you, your guns and a galaxy of robots.
