Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the Cracked game for Android (package com.cracked.pro), made by Rabi Dahal. In short: the game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts of its own. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google, and only if you sign in to Google Play Games.

Data kept on your phone

  • Progress: the highest level you've unlocked and your best score on each level.
  • Settings: things like look sensitivity, volume and control layout.

These are stored in the game's private storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere by the game itself, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses Google's Play Games Services to:

  • show your Play Games gamer name in the game;
  • keep a cloud copy of your progress (unlocked level and best scores) in Play Games saved games, so it carries across devices;
  • if the game has them, submit your total score to a leaderboard and unlock achievements.

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer can see aggregate statistics and leaderboard entries in the Google Play Console, but the game never receives your email address or contacts. You can play without signing in, and you can manage or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Google Play

If you install Cracked from Google Play, Google may collect crash reports and install statistics as part of the Play Store. That is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Cracked is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
